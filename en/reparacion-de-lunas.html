Windscreen and Glass Repair

A broken window is the most common claim on a motor policy and, on most policies, does not affect your no-claims bonus.

How it works

The glass service covers repairing or replacing the windscreen and the vehicle's other windows. When the damage is a small chip, repairing it is quicker and cheaper than replacing the glass, and on many policies it is done with no excess.

Let us know before you go to the garage: we'll check exactly what your policy covers, open the claim and confirm which approved centre applies to you.

A small chip can be repaired; if the crack grows or sits in the driver's field of view, the glass has to be replaced. Don't wait: a crack that spreads turns a cheap repair into a full replacement.

The service includes

  • Windscreen
  • Side and rear windows
  • Sunroof
  • Mirrors
  • Locks
  • Bodywork
  • Undercarriage parts
