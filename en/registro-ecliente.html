How it works

  • 1. Fill in the requestWe need your name, your ID/passport number, an email address and a contact phone number. With that we can find your client record.
  • 2. We verify your detailsWe check that the details match those on your policies. If anything is missing, we'll call you.
  • 3. You receive your login detailsWe email you your username and password, which you can change the first time you log in.

What you'll be able to do

  • Your policies always to handView and download the terms and certificates for each policy.
  • Payments and receiptsCheck the status of your payments and their due dates.
  • Claim reportReport what happened and track it from the same place.
  • Available 24 hours a dayNo need to work around office hours, from your phone or computer.
