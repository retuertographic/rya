Personalised Comparison

Not just one quote: several companies compared on the same need, with the cover, limits and excesses set out side by side.

The best backing for the right decision

Comparing well isn't comparing prices

Comparing insurance on price alone is the fastest way to end up badly covered. Two policies costing the same can have very different limits, excesses and exclusions, and that only comes to light on the day of the claim.

Our comparison sets out, side by side, what actually matches: what each company covers, up to what amount, with what excess, and what it leaves out. With that in front of you, the decision is yours, and it's an informed one.

What the comparison includes

  • Equivalent coverWe compare cover item by item, not just price against price.
  • Limits and sub-limitsThe sum insured and the caps on each cover, which is where the surprises usually are.
  • ExcessesHow much stays on you in each claim and how that affects the premium.
  • ExclusionsWhat neither policy covers, spelled out clearly.
  • Insurer solvencyWe look at the most reputable and financially sound insurers in each line.
  • Service qualityHow each insurer behaves when a claim has to be handled.
