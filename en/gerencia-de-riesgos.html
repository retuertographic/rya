Risk management

The risk management process identifies, analyses and assesses the risks a business faces in carrying out its activity, in order to reduce them, eliminate them or transfer them to third parties.

Risk management

Reduce, eliminate or transfer risk?

Protect your employees, your company's assets and its financial success by protecting them from risk.

Not every risk needs cover: some can be eliminated by changing a process, others are reduced with protective measures, and only what remains is transferred to an insurer. That order is what keeps the premium down and stops a claim from catching you without cover.

What it protects

  • Your employees
  • The company's assets and property
  • Business continuity
  • The company's financial success

The process, step by step
