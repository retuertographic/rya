About Us

Retuerto y Asociados, Insurance Brokers, started trading in 1992 under the direction of Ricardo Retuerto Moreno.

The key to our success: teamwork

Celebrating 30 years

More than thirty years supporting families, shops and businesses across the Canary Islands with everything to do with their insurance.

Watch «30th Anniversary, Retuerto y Asociados, Insurance Brokers» on YouTube

Who we are

We currently have more than ten professionals on our team — insurance technicians, economists and specialists in specific fields — who provide qualified advice on insurance matters, with a structure that lets us cover the whole archipelago.

Our aim

To offer our clients different insurance solutions suited to their needs, with the best quality and price, providing guidance and information on the terms of taking out cover.

To do this, we analyse the most reputable and financially sound insurers in each segment of the market, and we have built a structure that allows us to handle clients and their needs comprehensively, with the same responsiveness for businesses and individuals alike.

Our role as brokers

The insurance broker is the expert who acts as an intermediary between the insurer and the client, representing the insured party and their interests, rather than the insurer alone.

We handle everything to do with the policy: from taking it out through to the claim, should one arise, defending the client's interests just as a solicitor would.

All classes of insurance

We work across all classes of insurance: property, life, personal and health, as well as funeral insurance, pension plans and retirement and savings products. In addition to insurance advice, we also offer other types of guidance, such as risk management and non-insurance products.
