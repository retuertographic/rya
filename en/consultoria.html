Consultancy for Businesses

Alongside insurance advice, we offer other kinds of consultancy, such as risk management and non-insurance products.

Risk management

The risk management process identifies, analyses and assesses the risks a business faces in the course of its activity, with the aim of reducing, eliminating or transferring them to third parties.

The goal is to protect employees, the company's assets and its economic success.

Risk and insurance consultancy

We review your current insurance programme, spot duplicate or insufficient cover, and propose the right insurance solution for each case.

We advise you on how to secure the right cover and on non-insurance products that complement your risk management.

Cyber security

Any business or self-employed professional that uses email, accepts card payments, handles personal data, has remote workers or has staff using mobile devices for work carries an insurable risk.

Cyber risk insurance covers incident response, data loss, business interruption and liability to third parties.
