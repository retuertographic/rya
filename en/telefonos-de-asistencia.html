Assistance Phone Numbers

When a claim happens, the first thing to do is call. Here's who to call, and in what order.

Roadside assistance

Call the assistance number of your motor policy's insurer: it appears on the back of your payment receipt, in your policy schedule and in your e-client area. Have your registration plate, policy number and exact location to hand.

  • Registration plate and policy number
  • Exact location and condition of the vehicle
  • If anyone is injured, call 112 first

Home assistance

For a domestic emergency — a water leak, an electrical fault, a lock — use the assistance number on your home or community policy. If the damage can be contained (turning off the water supply, switching off the power), do so before calling.

  • Policy number and address
  • What happened and since when
  • Photos of the damage, if possible

Medical assistance

Your health policy will have an emergency number and an authorisations number. For a life-threatening emergency, always call 112 first.

  • Your health insurer's card
  • Nearest affiliated centre
  • Prior authorisation if the test requires it

Business and shop claims

Call us. In a multi-risk, liability or transport claim, how the report is opened and documented shapes everything that follows.

  • Date, time and cause of the claim
  • Estimated extent of the damage
  • Invoices, delivery notes and photos
