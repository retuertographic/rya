Sixth information note on the activity of the CCS regarding the volcanic eruption on the island of La Palma

Cumbre Vieja volcano, La Palma

As of 7 October, the CCS (Consorcio de Compensación de Seguros, Spain's Insurance Compensation Consortium) has paid 3,716,198 Euros to insured parties affected by the volcanic eruption on the island of La Palma. The payments, made by bank transfer to the policyholders, correspond to seventeen homes and two commercial premises that have been destroyed by the lava.

SIXTH INFORMATION NOTE ON THE ACTIVITY OF THE CCS REGARDING THE VOLCANIC ERUPTION ON THE ISLAND OF LA PALMA

  1. As of 7 October, the CCS has paid €3,716,198 to insured parties affected by the volcanic eruption on the island of La Palma. The payments, made by bank transfer to the policyholders, correspond to seventeen homes and two commercial premises that have been destroyed by the lava.

  1. So far, the CCS has received 556 compensation claims, corresponding to 423 homes, 96 motor vehicles, 34 shops and 3

By area of origin, 363 compensation claims come from Los Llanos de Aridane; 131 claims from El Paso; 39 from Tazacorte; 10 from Breña Alta; 5 from Santa Cruz de La Palma; 4 from Fuencaliente de La Palma, and another 4 from Villa de Mazo.

  1. All claims have been immediately passed on to the team of loss adjusters appointed to assess the compensable damage as they are received, and the loss adjusters, in turn, are contacting policyholders within no more than 48 hours of the date their claim was filed.

  1. 69% of compensation claims have been filed through the CCS website (consorseguros.es), 30% by calling the CCS helpline (freephone number 900 222 665) and only a residual 1% have reached the CCS on paper.

Insurers have requested compensation on behalf of affected policyholders in 54% of cases; insurance intermediaries (agents or brokers) in 33% of cases; and the policyholder has done so directly in the remaining 13% of cases.

In the case of motor vehicles, homes and homeowners' associations, the CCS will pay the amount corresponding to the full compensable damage under the affected party's insurance contract. In the case of shops, industrial premises and civil works, the CCS deducts an excess set by law at 7% of the compensable damage, which is borne by the policyholder; this means that the CCS will pay €93 for every €100 of compensable damage in shops, industrial premises and civil works, whatever the total amount of the damage.
