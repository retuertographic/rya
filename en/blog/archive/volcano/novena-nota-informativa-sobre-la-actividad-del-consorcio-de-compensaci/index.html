Ninth Information Notice on the Activity of the Consorcio de Compensación de Seguros (CCS) Following the Volcanic Eruption on the Island of La Palma

Cumbre Vieja Volcano, La Palma

As of 22 November, the CCS has paid out €42,487,217 to policyholders affected by the volcanic eruption on the island of La Palma.

This amount, in turn, corresponds to 185 homes, 20 vehicles, 8 shops and office premises and 1 industrial facility. All of these risks are located in the municipalities of Los Llanos de Aridane, El Paso and Tazacorte.

  1. As of the same date, the CCS has received 2,209 compensation claims, for damage to 1,808 homes, 187 motor vehicles, 199 shops, hotels and office premises, and 15 industrial facilities. By locality, the most notable are Los Llanos de Aridane, with 1,415 claims; El Paso, with a total of 396 claims; Tazacorte, with 102 claims; Santa Cruz de La Palma, with 81 claims; Breña Alta, with 74; Breña Baja, with 50; and Villa de Mazo, with 22.
  1.   1.   1. 54% of the 2,209 compensation claims were submitted via the CCS website (consorseguros.es), and the remaining 46% by calling the CCS telephone helpline (freephone 900 222 665). Insurers, acting on behalf of affected policyholders, requested compensation in 45% of cases; insurance intermediaries (agents or brokers) did so in 32% of cases; and the policyholder made the request directly in the remaining 23% of cases.

Madrid, 22 November 2021
