What is a broker?

Publications

▶️ What IS a broker?

✅ A personal insurance advisor who analyses the risk and the market to offer you the insurance solution that best fits your real needs.

✅ They offer you a full service, by your side from the moment you take out the policy through to any claim.

✅ They defend your interests as a client and insurance consumer before insurance companies whenever you need it.

✅ They are a qualified, expert and experienced professional, committed to ongoing training.

▶️What is NOT a broker?

❌ A "mere intermediary" for insurance

❌ Someone who "drives up the price" of your policy. Using a broker never means higher premiums for the insured.

❌ A "bureaucratic nuisance" — they are the person who's always on your side.

❌They don't sell products from a single insurer; they are the only truly independent professionals in the sector, so there's no conflict of interest.
