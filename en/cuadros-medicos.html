Medical Networks

A medical network is the list of practitioners, clinics and affiliated centres you can visit with your health policy.

What a medical network is

Each company has its own network and updates it frequently, so before booking an appointment it's worth checking that the practitioner is still affiliated, and on what terms — whether prior authorisation is needed or the consultation involves a co-payment.

If you're already our client, tell us which specialty and area you need and we'll send you your insurer's up-to-date medical network. And if you're choosing a policy, we'll compare the medical networks of the leading insurers for your area.

Before booking an appointment

  • Check it's still affiliatedMedical networks change: a centre can stop being affiliated from one year to the next.
  • Check whether authorisation is neededDiagnostic tests and hospital admission usually require prior authorisation from the insurer.
  • Keep your card handyPolicy number and insurer card, physical or from the app.
  • Ask about the co-paymentUnder co-payment plans, each medical visit has a fixed charge.
